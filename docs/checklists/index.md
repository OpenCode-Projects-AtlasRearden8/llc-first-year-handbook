# The checklists

This is the printable layer of the handbook — the pages you'll actually live from once the chapters have taught you the *why*. Print them, or keep them open on a second screen during each ritual.

| Checklist | When you use it | The chapter behind it |
|---|---|---|
| [First 30 days](first-30-days.md) | Once, at launch — formation to fully running | Everything |
| [The money date](quarterly-money-date.md) | Four times a year, ~90 minutes | [Quarterly money dates](../money-dates.md) |
| [Year end](year-end.md) | November–December, once a year | [Taxes](../taxes.md), [records](../records.md) |

How they're built:

- **Checkbox items only** — `[ ]` boxes you pen a checkmark into. No prose required beyond a stroke.
- **Print one copy per use** — the money-date one especially. A filled checklist from this quarter is the *input* to next quarter's date.
- **Sequence preserved** — each checklist is ordered by dependency. Start at the top.
- **Gentle versions included** — where a ritual can shrink on a low-energy day, the checklist says exactly how small it may get and still count.

The handbook's companion pieces — *small-business-ai-playbook* for AI-era habits and *cashflow-simple-ledger* for the ledger tool itself — have worksheets of their own; these three are the LLC's administrative spine.
