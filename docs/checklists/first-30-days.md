# First 30 days

The launch checklist — from formation to fully running. Roughly four sittings: one evening for the state/federal identity, one morning for the bank, one evening for insurance, one low-energy hour for the housekeeping. Nothing here needs a professional unless a [trigger](../professionals.md) fires.

## Sitting one: the identity (60–90 min)

- [ ] **Find the formation proof.** Articles of Organization PDF (from the [Indiana INBiz](https://inbiz.in.gov/) filing) saved to the LLC's `legal/` folder. Can't find it? The SOS portal has it under your business record — download it now, this hour, not someday.
- [ ] **Create the folder system of the year** exactly as the [record-habits chapter](../records.md) lays it out: `2026-Q4`-style quarter folders plus `legal/` and `taxes/`.
- [ ] **Apply for the EIN** at [IRS.gov](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) — free, ~15 minutes. **Download the CP 575 letter the moment it appears** and save it to `legal/` (twice: two backups).
- [ ] **Choose-or-confirm the registered agent:** me (with an Indiana street address I can defend) or a commercial agent. Note it in `legal/`. (Weighing the privacy trade? [the annual-report ritual](../annual-report.md).)
- [ ] **Test drive the business search:** find your LLC on the [INBiz business search](https://bsd.sos.in.gov/publicbusinesssearch), note your **anniversary month** and whether a Business Entity Report is due this year or next. One line in the folder: *"BER due: ___"*
- [ ] **Bookmark, in one bookmarks folder named the LLC:** [INBiz filings](https://inbiz.in.gov/business-filings), [SOS entity reports](https://www.in.gov/sos/business/business-entity-reports), [IRS estimated taxes](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/estimated-taxes), [IRS Direct Pay](https://www.irs.gov/payments/direct-pay), [Indiana DOR](https://www.in.gov/dor/)

## Sitting two: the bank (90 min)

- [ ] **Call ahead** and ask what they need — then bring the [full expected stack](../bank-accounts.md): EIN letter, Articles, one-page operating agreement, your ID, deposit money.
- [ ] **Open pot 1** — business checking. Note the CSV-export answer in the notes field of the ledger.
- [ ] **Open pot 2** — savings/second sub-account with **no card attached**, for tax money only.
- [ ] **Order or activate only the business card.** Personal card goes back in the wallet with a new rule attached: business purchases from pot 1, every time, no exceptions.
- [ ] **Create the pay-yourself ritual:** a recurring twice-monthly calendar nudge named "pay yourself + tax pot."
- [ ] **Set up (or import into) the ledger** with both accounts connected: *cashflow-simple-ledger* is the house tool; a spreadsheet honors the habit too.

## Sitting three: insurance + professionals (one evening)

- [ ] **The quoting evening** from [insurance starters](../insurance.md): GL quote → BOP quote → E&O conversation → personal health/disability gap noted.
- [ ] **Pick an agent** (independent, multi-carrier preferred) and *say the words* "one-person LLC, publishing and small tools" — the classification powers the quote.
- [ ] **File the COI PDF** to `legal/insurance/` the day it issues; renewal dates into the calendar.
- [ ] **Identify one tax professional** (EA, CPA, or preparer) *before* the first estimate season — draft the first-call script from [when to call a pro](../professionals.md) once, while nothing is urgent.

## Sitting four: the calendar, and the quiet parts (60 min, low energy is fine)

- [ ] **Plant the year's dates:** four [quarterly money dates](../money-dates.md), four [estimate due dates](../taxes.md) (April/June/September/January + 15), and the annual **LLC ritual** in the anniversary month.
- [ ] **Set reminders at T-1 week and T-1 day** for the estimate dates at minimum.
- [ ] **Choose the set-aside percentage** for the tax pot (round numbers: the [taxes chapter](../taxes.md)); note it on the money-date checklist.
- [ ] **Phone receipt habit:** photos straight into the current quarter folder, or into an app that syncs there. One test photo: go take one right now, three seconds.
- [ ] **BOI note in the ritual page:** *"checked at launch (late 2026): U.S. companies exempt per FinCEN's August 2026 final rule — re-verify per [FinCEN](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) each year."*
- [ ] **Write the one-sentence purpose of the LLC** at the top of the folder: what it sells, for whom. It sounds cheesy; every future decision gets faster.

## The joy item (not optional, not scheduled)

- [ ] **Celebrate the launch.** Something small, deliberately purchased from pot 1, recorded in the ledger with the category that says so. First-manual joy counts as a business expense the way printer ink doesn't.

When every box has a check, the LLC is *running* — and everything after this page is rhythm: [the weekly ten minutes](../records.md), the [quarterly date](../quarterly-money-date.md), the [yearly ritual](../annual-report.md).
