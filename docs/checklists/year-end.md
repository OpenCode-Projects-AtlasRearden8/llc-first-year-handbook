# Year end

The once-a-year wind-down, run mostly *from* the Q4 [money date](../money-dates.md) in November or early December. Its purpose: make tax season a review instead of a scramble, and hand next year a clean slate. Everything here uses records you already keep — if a box is still scary, the [record habits](../records.md) will catch its cause for next year.

## The books wrap (45 min)

- [ ] Ledger reconciled through the year's last week — bank, card, and ledger agree
- [ ] `Uncategorized` swept to zero (or knowingly documented)
- [ ] **Profit for the year, computed:** $ ____________
- [ ] Mileage log tallied for the year; note field total: ________ miles
- [ ] Receipt photos all present in the four quarterly folders (missing ones chased and filed)
- [ ] The [cashflow tax-season export](../cashflow-simple-ledger) for the year generated and filed into `taxes/` — the clean CSV a tax professional would want
- [ ] Backup verified (open a file from the backup; read it; confirm alive)

## The tax-pot reality check (30 min)

- [ ] **Tax pot balance vs. what the [round-number rule](../taxes.md) expects for the year's profit:** ____________ vs ____________
- [ ] Estimated payments made during the year, listed with dates: ______________________
- [ ] The **January 15 estimate** (for Q4) scheduled or paid — pot 2 funds it, visibly
- [ ] Decision made: who prepares the return — me with the ledger, or the [tax professional](../professionals.md)? If a pro: first meeting (or document hand-off) **booked before January 15**
- [ ] Anything that changed mid-year (new income type, new state contacts, life events) listed as one of the *first* things told to whoever prepares

## The calendar replant (30 min)

- [ ] Next year's four [money dates](../money-dates.md) planted (same day-type as this year worked — keep it if so)
- [ ] Next year's four estimate dates planted (April / June / September / January + 15), with T-1 week and T-1 day reminders
- [ ] The annual [LLC ritual](../annual-report.md) planted in the anniversary month — and the *actual* Indiana Business Entity Report due year checked once against the [INBiz business search](https://bsd.sos.in.gov/publicbusinesssearch)
- [ ] Insurance renewal dates visible for next year ([insurance starters](../insurance.md))
- [ ] One theme chosen for next year (from the money-date goal notes): ______________________

## The housekeeping hour (optional, lovely)

- [ ] Paper archive scanned into the folder tree; originals filed in the physical `legal/` binder
- [ ] Old quarter folders closed: a folder is "closed" when a ten-second glance finds nothing loose
- [ ] Subscription list one final cull before renewals tick to next year
- [ ] `legal/` folder contents verified against the [first-30-day launch list](first-30-days.md) — EIN letter, Articles, COI, agent note, all present
- [ ] Thank-you notes: one client/reader/partner you were glad existed. Send one. (Costs a stamp or nothing; returns for years.)

## And then: stop working until the new year's money date

The year-end checklist exists so **January is quiet.** If this page is fully checked by mid-December, the next significant money event is the [Q1 money date](quarterly-money-date.md), which starts from a filled and beautiful pair of pages. That calm handoff is the actual output of year one.

*Orientation only — never legal, tax, or financial advice. Year-end steps that touch tax decisions — estimate amounts, deduction positions, gray areas — are decided with a professional when stakes or uncertainty rise.*
